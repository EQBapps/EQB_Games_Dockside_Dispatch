Dockside Dispatch

A relaxing container-sorting puzzle set in harbours around the world. Tap a container with a clear path to the quay and the crane loads it onto the ship of its colour. Fill a ship and it sails away. No ship for that colour yet? The container waits in the holding bay, but don't let the bay fill up.

Two ways to play

Load the waiting ships from the container yard, or Unload a full ship onto trucks of matching colours and watch them drive off down the dock road.

A twist in every port

Long containers fill two slots. Locked containers open when the one carrying their key is sent off. Mystery containers show their colour once they have a clear path out. When you get stuck, Extra Berth, Crane Lift and Undo help you out.

The studio

Small games, made with care
