Welcome to the docks! Dockside Dispatch is a calm, colourful puzzle about thinking one move ahead: send every container to the ship of its colour, use the holding bay wisely, and keep the harbour moving.

EASY TO START, REWARDING TO MASTER
• Tap a container with a clear path to the quay to load it onto the ship of its colour. Full ships sail away.
• No ship for that colour yet? Park the container in the holding bay, but don't let the bay fill up!
• Every 5th level is a Hard level that rewards planning ahead, with bonus coins when you clear it.
• No timers: take as long as you like over each move.

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
• LOAD: fill the waiting ships from the container yard.
• UNLOAD: a ship has come in full. Lift each container onto the truck of its colour and watch full trucks drive off. Unlocks after level 5.

A TWIST IN EVERY PORT
• Long containers fill two slots, so plan where they go.
• Locked containers open when the container carrying their key is sent off.
• Mystery containers show their colour once they have a clear path out.

PLAY TOGETHER ON THE BIG SCREEN
• Plays on Android TV and Google TV with just the remote, with no ads on TV.
• Fills the screen in landscape on tablets and Chromebooks.
• A relaxed game to share at home: one person plays, everyone helps plan the next move.

HANDY BOOSTERS
• Extra Berth adds space to the holding bay.
• Crane Lift plucks a boxed-in container straight out of the stack.
• Undo takes back your last move, even the one that filled the bay.

SAIL AROUND THE WORLD
Visit 20 famous ports, a new one every 5 levels: Hong Kong, Singapore, Dubai, Mumbai, Amsterdam, Los Angeles, Sydney, Rotterdam, Shanghai, New York, London, Hamburg, Tokyo, San Francisco, Rio de Janeiro, Cape Town, Istanbul, Venice, Barcelona and Seattle. The harbour follows your clock, from sunrise to starry nights.

• 200 hand-tuned levels, 100 in each mode
• Colour-blind friendly: every colour has its own symbol
• Relaxing sounds and a cosy, colourful art style
• Play offline, anytime

Free to play, with optional ads. One optional purchase, Dockside Premium, removes the ads between levels on every device with your Google account. On Android TV the first 15 levels of each mode are free, and Premium unlocks the rest.
