A calm, colourful puzzle to play together at home. Dockside Dispatch is about thinking one move ahead: send every container to the ship of its colour, decide what waits in the holding bay, and keep the harbour moving. Easy to pick up for a family game night, with plenty to think about as the levels grow.

PLAY TOGETHER ON THE BIG SCREEN
• Plays on Android TV and Google TV with just the remote, with no ads on TV.
• One person holds the remote, everyone helps plan the next move.
• Also on phones, tablets and Chromebooks, so the game goes wherever you do.

A GAME THAT REWARDS PLANNING
• No timers and no rush: take as long as you like over each move.
• Every 5th level is a Hard level where planning ahead makes the difference.
• Undo takes back a move, so trying out an idea is never a problem.
• Colour-blind friendly: every colour has its own symbol.

HOW TO PLAY
• LOAD: tap a container with a clear path to load it onto the ship of its colour. Full ships sail away.
• No ship for that colour yet? Park it in the holding bay, but don't let the bay fill up!
• UNLOAD: lift containers out of a full ship onto the trucks of their colour. Unlocks after level 5.
• New twists along the way: long containers, locked containers with keys, and mystery containers.

SAIL AROUND THE WORLD
20 famous ports, from Hong Kong and Mumbai to Venice and Seattle, a new one every 5 levels. The harbour follows your clock, from sunrise to starry nights.

• 200 hand-tuned levels, 100 in each mode
• Relaxing sounds and a cosy, colourful art style
• Play offline, anytime

Free to play, with optional ads on phones and tablets, and no ads on TV. One optional purchase, Dockside Premium, removes the ads between levels on every device with your Google account. On Android TV the first 15 levels of each mode are free, and Premium unlocks the rest.
