Easy to start. Every 5th level fights back. Dockside Dispatch is a container-sorting puzzle where the real challenge is planning: which container goes now, which one waits in the holding bay, and which move fills the bay a turn too early.

THE PUZZLE
• Tap a container with a clear path to load it onto the ship of its colour. Full ships sail away.
• No ship for that colour yet? It waits in the holding bay. Fill the bay and the level is lost.
• Every 5th level is a Hard level: fewer berths, fuller yards, bonus coins when you clear it.
• No timers. Every level is solvable; the question is whether you see the way through.

TWISTS THAT CHANGE YOUR PLAN
• Long containers take two slots, on a ship or in the bay.
• Locked containers stay put until their key container is sent off.
• Mystery containers hide their colour until they have a clear path out.

TWO MODES
• LOAD: fill the ships from the container yard.
• UNLOAD: empty a full ship onto the trucks waiting on the dock road. Unlocks after level 5.

BOOSTERS FOR WHEN YOU'RE STUCK
• Extra Berth: one more holding-bay slot.
• Crane Lift: pull a boxed-in container straight out.
• Undo: take back your last move.
