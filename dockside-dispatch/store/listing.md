## Text

**App name** (30 characters max): Dockside Dispatch

**Short description** (80 characters max):
> Sort colourful containers onto ships in a relaxing harbour puzzle!

**Full description** (4,000 characters max):

> Welcome to the docks! Load colourful containers onto the waiting ships, clear the yard and send each ship sailing off into the sunset.
>
> **HOW TO PLAY**
> • Tap a container with a clear path to the quay to load it onto the ship of the same colour.
> • No ship for that colour yet? It waits in the holding bay, but don't let the bay fill up!
> • Fill a ship and it sails away, making room for the next one.
>
> **A TWIST IN EVERY PORT**
> • Long containers fill two slots, so plan where they go.
> • Locked containers open when the container carrying their key is sent off.
> • Mystery containers show their colour once they have a clear path out.
>
> **HANDY BOOSTERS**
> • Extra Berth adds space to the holding bay.
> • Crane Lift plucks a boxed-in container straight out of the stack.
> • Shuffle mixes the colours in the yard.
>
> **SAIL AROUND THE WORLD**
> Sail to 20 famous ports, a new one every 5 levels: Hong Kong, Singapore, Dubai, Mumbai, Amsterdam, Los Angeles, Sydney, Rotterdam, Shanghai, New York, London, Hamburg, Tokyo, San Francisco, Rio de Janeiro, Cape Town, Istanbul, Venice, Barcelona and Seattle. The harbour follows your clock, from sunrise to starry nights.
>
> • 100 hand-tuned levels that get trickier as you go
> • Colour-blind friendly: every colour has its own symbol
> • Relaxing sounds and a cosy, colourful art style
> • Play offline, anytime
>
> Free to play, with optional ads.
