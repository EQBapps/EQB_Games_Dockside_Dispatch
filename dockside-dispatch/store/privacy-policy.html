Dockside Dispatch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 December 2026 · Applies to the Dockside Dispatch game for Android and iOS

The short version

No account needed
You can play without signing up. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
Your progress stays on your device
Your levels, coins, boosters and settings are saved on your phone, not on our servers. If you use Google Play Games, your achievements are also saved to your Play Games profile.
Ads and analytics partners collect some data
The free game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and measures gameplay with GameAnalytics. These partners collect device and usage information, as explained below. On Android TV the game shows no ads.
One optional purchase
Dockside Premium removes the ads between levels and unlocks the full game on Android TV. Google Play or the App Store handles the payment; we never see your payment details.
You are in control
You can turn personalised ads on or off and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information at any time in the game: open the pause menu and choose Privacy.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device so it can remember where you are. We do not receive this information.

  • Your current level in each game mode (Load and Unload), coins and booster counts.
  • Your settings: sound on or off, reminders on or off, and your privacy choices.
  • Whether you own Dockside Premium (the store confirms this again each time the game starts).
  • Which tutorial tips you have already seen.

The game also checks your device's region setting, on your device only, to decide whether to ask for your consent to personalised ads (see section 6). Deleting the game removes all of this information.

3. Information collected through our partners

Dockside Dispatch is free to play. To fund it and to improve it, we use the following partners, whose software is built into the game. They collect information automatically when you play.

Advertising: Unity LevelPlay

We show ads, including optional "watch a video" rewards and occasional ads between levels, through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service from Unity Technologies (including ironSource Ads and Unity Ads). LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID (Google's Advertising ID on Android; on iPhone, Apple's Identifier for Advertisers, only if you allow tracking).
  • Device and connection information: device model, operating system and version, language, mobile carrier, IP address and an approximate location derived from it (country or city level).
  • Information about ads: which ads were requested, shown, clicked or completed, and the revenue an ad earned.

On Android TV the game shows no ads, and the advertising software described here does not start.

They use this information to choose and show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalise ads. Personalised ads use information about your interests, which may come from your activity in other apps. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

The ad networks that LevelPlay works with in this game, each with its own privacy policy:

  • ironSource Ads and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): privacy policy
  • Google AdMob (Google, Android): how Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms):privacy policy
  • Liftoff Monetize (Liftoff Mobile): privacy policy
  • Mintegral: privacy policy
  • InMobi: privacy policy

Analytics: GameAnalytics

We use GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played, so we can make levels fair and fix problems. GameAnalytics may collect:

  • A random identifier for your installation, and device identifiers, which may include your advertising ID.
  • Device information: device model, operating system, app version, language and country.
  • Gameplay events: when sessions start and end, the game mode you play, levels started, completed or failed, boosters used, coins earned and spent, ads shown, the port you reached, and whether a Premium purchase was started, completed or cancelled (never your payment details).

See the GameAnalytics privacy notice.

3a. Google Play Games (Android)

If you use Google Play Games on your device, the game signs you in to it automatically so you can earn achievements. The game then sends your achievement progress (for example levels completed and containers dispatched) to Google, so it appears in your Play Games profile. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy and your Play Games settings. We do not receive your name, email address or other Google account details. You can play without Play Games: if you are not signed in, your progress is kept on your device only.

3b. Game Center (iOS)

On iPhone, if you are signed in to Game Center, the game uses it for achievements. The game sends your achievement progress (for example levels completed and containers dispatched) to Apple, so it appears in Game Center. Apple handles this under the Apple Privacy Policy and your Game Center settings. We do not receive your name, email address or other Apple Account details. You can play without Game Center: if you are not signed in, your progress is kept on your device only.

3c. Purchases (Dockside Premium)

Dockside Premium is an optional one-time purchase. It removes the ads between levels and unlocks every level on Android TV. Purchases are made through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS), which collect and process your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy or the Apple Privacy Policy. We never receive your card or other payment details.

To unlock Premium, the game receives confirmation from the store that your store account has bought it, together with the store's order details for that purchase (such as an order ID and the time of purchase). The game asks the store again each time it starts, so Premium carries over to other devices that use the same store account, and a refunded purchase is removed. This check happens between the game and the store; the order details are not sent to our servers.

6. Your choices

Personalised ads consent (EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If your device's region is in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, the game asks the first time you play whether our ad partners may use information from your device to personalise ads. No ads load until you answer. If you choose "No", you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change your answer at any time: open the pause menu, choose Privacy and switch Personalised ads on or off.

"Do not sell or share my info" (United States)

In the pause menu, choose Privacy and switch on Do not sell/share my info. We then tell our ad partners to stop using your information for personalised advertising ("sale" or "sharing" under California and other US state laws).

Reminders

If you agree, the game schedules a few reminder notifications on your device (at most one a day, during the daytime, and none after a week away). They are created and shown by your device; no information is sent to us or our partners to do this. You can switch reminders off in the pause menu, or turn off notifications for the game in your device's settings.

Device settings

  • Android: Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads.
  • iOS: if the game asks to track you, you can choose "Ask App Not to Track", and change this later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Deleting the game stops all collection by the game and its partners.

7. Children

Dockside Dispatch is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will work with our partners to delete it.

8. International transfers

Our partners may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where information from the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred, our partners rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions, as described in their privacy policies.

9. How long information is kept

Progress and settings stay on your device until you delete the game or its data. Our partners keep the information they collect for as long as they need it for the purposes above, as set out in their own policies. We ask GameAnalytics to keep analytics data for no longer than 24 months.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for access to, or a copy of, information about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, how it is used.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising, and not be treated differently for using your rights.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

India. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask for a summary of your personal data and how it is processed, ask us to correct, complete, update or erase it, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf, and have your grievance addressed by our Grievance Officer (section 13). If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Because the game has no accounts, we usually cannot identify you from your request alone. To help us find information held by our partners, please include your device's advertising ID or the GameAnalytics identifier if you can. Contact us at contact@eqbsolutions.com. You can also contact our partners directly using the links in section 3.

12. Changes to this policy
