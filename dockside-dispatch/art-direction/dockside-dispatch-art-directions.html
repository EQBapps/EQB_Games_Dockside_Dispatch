Dockside Dispatch: game scene art directions

Four looks for the same moment in the same level: three ships at their berths, the crane lifting a container onto the nearly full yellow ship, 2 of 5 holding slots used, and a container yard with long, locked and hidden-colour containers. Only the art direction changes.

The same in every option

  • Colour-blind support: each container colour also carries its own symbol (circle, triangle, square, diamond).
  • Readable yard: the top row, which the player can tap now, is full brightness; blocked rows behind it are dimmed.
  • Ad-ready frame: the crane mid-lift shows the core action in a single image.

Recommendation

Lead with A · Golden Hour Harbour and test B · Toy Box Diorama against it, using a short video ad in each style as the first install-cost test. Keep C as a possible seasonal theme (a night-port event) rather than the main look.
